Privacy Policy: Games by Joy Barua

Overview

Our games don't ask for your name, email or any account, unless a game's entry under Games covered says otherwise. They show ads and use analytics and crash reporting from Google, which collect some device and usage information as described below.

This policy applies to the iOS and Android games listed under Games covered below, made by Joy Barua ("we", "us"). Effective date: October 26, 2026.

Information we collect

Stored only on your device. Your game progress and settings, such as levels completed, scores, coins and sound on/off, stay on your phone or tablet. We never receive them. Deleting a game deletes them.

Collected by Google services in our games.

We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or payment information.

How information is used and shared

We use this information only to run our games, show ads, measure how the game is used, and fix problems.

  • Google receives the information listed above as our service provider for ads, analytics and crash reporting. Google's use is covered by its own privacy policy and its partner sites policy.
  • Advertisers in Google's ad network may receive limited information through AdMob when an ad is shown, as described in Google's policies.
  • We don't sell your information for money, and we don't share it with anyone else except when required by law.

Nothing is logged before the consent step finishes. Where consent is required, Google services follow the choices you make in the consent form.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). Our games ask for your consent before ads or analytics run. You can change your choice any time with Privacy choices in each game's settings, opened from the gear button on its Home screen.
  • Tracking on iPhone and iPad. iOS asks whether a game may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your device's advertising ID isn't used, and ads aren't personalized based on tracking. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Advertising ID on Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Delete your data. Uninstalling a game deletes its progress on your device. To ask about deleting analytics or crash data, email us (see Contact). Because our games have no accounts, we may need your help to identify your device's data.

Retention, children and your rights

Retention. Game progress stays on your device until you delete the game. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months after you last play. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

Children. Our games are not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used one of our games and you want their data removed, contact us and we'll help.

Security. Information sent to Google services travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of storage or transfer is completely secure.

International transfers. Google may process information in the United States and other countries where it operates.

Privacy rights. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, or US states such as California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or limit its use, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Use the choices above, or email us, and we'll respond within the time the law requires.
